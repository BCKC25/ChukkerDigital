What each plan includes every month

Each plan includes everything in the plan before it.

Tier 1

Maintain & Protect

For a site that works and customers who already find you.

  • Software, plugin and security updates
  • Weekly backups and uptime monitoring, so we know if your site goes down
  • Up to 3 small text or photo changes a month (unused changes don't roll over)
  • A monthly check-in email from Google Analytics: visitors, top pages and anything that needs attention
  • Broken-site fixes the same day

a month$150–$250

Tier 2

Grow & Optimize

For when people search "near me" and competitors show up first on Google Maps.

  • Up to 5 small text or photo changes a month, instead of 3
  • Local SEO: 2–3 target keywords picked each month, like "hair salon Aiken"
  • 2 new or refreshed pages or blog posts a month
  • Google Business Profile management: weekly posts, photo updates, accurate hours and help with reviews
  • A ranking snapshot in your monthly email: where you show up in Google search and Maps
  • A monthly "anything coming up?" note, so your events and promotions get used

a month$600–$1,000

Tier 3

The Complete Engine

For open spots on the calendar you want paid ads to fill.

  • Unlimited small text and photo changes
  • Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad setup and management
  • Weekly ad checks: spending pace, which searches trigger your ads, and turning off ads that aren't working
  • New ad wording and images tested every month
  • A monthly ROI report: ad spend, clicks, calls, bookings, leads and cost per lead
  • No change to your ad budget without your written OK

a month$1,500–$2,000+

Ground rules

Every plan includes

  1. One request form

    Send changes anytime and get a due date back. Most requests are done within 3 business days. See turnaround times.

  2. You own every account

    Your website, domain, Google and Meta accounts stay in your business's name. Chukker Digital gets access, never ownership.

  3. A monthly check-in in plain English

    What changed, what it brought in, and anything needed from you.

  4. No hourly billing

    One flat rate with a 3-month start, so SEO and ads have time to show results. Move up a plan anytime; the new rate starts next billing month.

  5. Ad spend and hosting are paid by you

    Straight to Google, Meta or your host. That money never passes through Chukker Digital.

Program notes

Questions about the plans

Which plan is right for my business?

If your site works and customers already find you, Maintain & Protect keeps it that way. If competitors show up ahead of you on Google Maps, Grow & Optimize adds local SEO and Google Business Profile work. If you have open spots to fill and want paid ads, The Complete Engine adds Google and Meta ads. The free audit will point to one.

Is there a contract?

Plans start with a 3-month period, because SEO and ads need time to show results. Your signed agreement lists your plan, monthly rate and payment terms.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. The plan price covers the work. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Google or Meta, and we never change your ad budget without your written OK.

What if I need something my plan doesn't cover?

You'll get a written quote before any work starts, and nothing is billed without your approval. If you need that kind of work every month, moving up a plan is usually the better deal.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade anytime, and the new rate starts with your next billing month.

Need a new website or a rebuild?

Monthly plans keep an existing site running and growing. Designing a new site or rebuilding an old one is a separate, one-time cost, quoted in writing before any work starts.
