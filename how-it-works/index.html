Rules of play

How working together works

Requests go through one simple form and are handled on a set schedule with published turnaround times. You always know where a request stands.

Requests

Every change goes through one simple form

You'll get a Request Form link to bookmark on your phone and computer. Texts and quick mentions in passing are easy to lose; the form means nothing does. Prefer email? Requests sent to requests@chukkerdigital.com go into the same queue.

  1. Name the page or platform

    For example "Home page" or "Google Business Profile".

  2. Describe the change

    Attach any new photos or text, and pick an urgency.

  3. Get a due date

    An email confirms it's received, with the date it'll be done.

  4. See it done

    A second email with a link or screenshot of the finished change.

Ground rules

Straightforward billing

  1. One invoice a month

    Sent at the start of each month for that month's work.

  2. Bank transfer or card

    No extra cost either way. Payment is due within 7 days of the invoice.

  3. Ads and hosting paid by you

    Directly to Google, Meta or your host, never through Chukker Digital.

  4. A 3-month start

    SEO and ads need time to show results. Move up a plan anytime; the new rate starts next billing month.

  5. Quotes before extra work

    Anything outside your plan gets a written quote first. Nothing is billed without your approval.

Program notes

Questions about working together

Can I just text you a change?

Texts are easy to lose, so you'll get a friendly reply asking you to send it through the Request Form, where it's tracked and gets a due date.

What happens if my site goes down?

Mark the request "Something is broken". Work starts within 4 hours and it's fixed the same day. Sites are also watched by uptime monitoring.

Do I have to share my passwords?

No. You add Chukker Digital as a user through each platform's own invitation, on a 20-minute call. Please never send passwords by text or email.

How do I pay?

An invoice is sent at the start of each month. Pay by bank transfer or card at no extra cost, within 7 days of the invoice.
