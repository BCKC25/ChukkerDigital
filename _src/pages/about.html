Program notes

Why "Chukker"?

A chukker is one period of play in a polo match, seven and a half minutes long. Aiken has been a polo town since the Winter Colony days, and the name is a nod to the horse community that shaped it. It also fits the work: steady, focused stretches of effort, month after month, rather than one big launch and silence.
