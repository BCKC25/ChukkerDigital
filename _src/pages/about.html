Josh Lovett, founder

I live and work in Aiken. For about a year before Chukker Digital was official, I ran the websites, Google listings and ads for a few local businesses, including my own. The same problem kept coming up: owner-run businesses were either paying agency prices for work they never saw, or doing it themselves late at night.

Chukker Digital is the fix I wanted for those businesses. One person who knows your website, your Google listing and your ads, works from a published schedule, and answers when you call.

I'm a Navy veteran, and I run client accounts the way I was trained to run equipment: a written checklist, every time. Every request is logged, every site is backed up before it's changed, and every client gets the same monthly check-in.

Why "Chukker"?

A chukker is one period of play in a polo match. Aiken has been a polo town since the Winter Colony days, and the name is a nod to the horse community that shaped it.

It also fits the work: steady, focused stretches of effort, month after month, rather than one big launch and silence.
